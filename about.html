ABOUT FCOM

Where computing meets impact

Established in 2013, the Faculty of Computing & Multimedia (FCOM) at Universiti Poly-Tech Malaysia offers a dynamic academic environment for students pursuing majors in Computer Science, Information Technology and Multimedia. Today FCOM runs 13 programmes from Foundation through to PhD, teaches roughly a fifth of UPTM's entire student body, and is built around a faculty where half of our lecturers are PhD holders or PhD candidates — so every programme stays grounded in active research as well as industry practice.

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Saifuddin bin Haji Mohtaram

Dean, Faculty of Computing & Multimedia
