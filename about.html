ABOUT FCOM

Where computing meets impact

The Faculty of Computing & Multimedia, established in 2013, offers a dynamic academic environment for students pursuing majors in Computer Science, Information Technology, and Multimedia. Since its inception, the faculty has been committed to providing high-quality education, equipping students with the skills and knowledge needed to excel in the rapidly evolving fields of technology and multimedia.

The faculty's programs are designed to foster critical thinking, problem-solving, and innovation, preparing graduates for various roles in the tech industry, from software development to multimedia design and IT management.

In addition to its academic offerings, the faculty is deeply engaged in research across a range of cutting-edge areas. These research initiatives include exploring the fundamentals of computer science, IT/IS applications, and networking. The faculty is also involved in advanced fields such as artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and software systems engineering. By actively contributing to these areas, the faculty not only enhances the academic experience for its students but also contributes to the broader advancement of technology and its applications in society.
